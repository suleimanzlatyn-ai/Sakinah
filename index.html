GOOD TO KNOW

Before you gather.

Helpful details for planning. For current availability and confirmed information, contact the restaurant directly.

How do I place an order?

Choose dishes from the menu, add them to your order and send the enquiry on WhatsApp. The restaurant must confirm actual prices, availability and collection or delivery options.

Is my table confirmed online?

No. The form sends a request to WhatsApp. Your table is confirmed only after the restaurant replies and confirms availability.

Are the menu and prices final?

Not yet. The demo uses sample items and prices. The owner should approve the real menu, prices and dish availability before launch.

Can I ask about a group booking?

Yes. Choose “Group / event enquiry” in the form and add your occasion and requirements in the notes.
